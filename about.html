Process

How the AI actually matches you

Three passes, each one narrowing toward a genuinely relevant connection — not just a keyword hit.

01

Understand

Resume, portfolio links, and preferences are parsed into a structured skill-and-intent profile — including adjacent skills and career trajectory.

02

Rank

Every open role is scored against that profile on fit, growth potential, and employer culture signals — not just matching job-title strings.

03

Connect

Only matches above a relevance threshold are surfaced to either side, with a plain-language explanation of why the match was made.
